Works 2026/03

Sagrado florecerSacred Blossoming

Patio del Museo de Bellas Artes y Museo Julio Romero de Torres

Work
03 of 05
Authors
Sofia Candeo in Art Syamhope, Azra Kaya and Carlo Marco Eurialo De Paoli
Venue
Patio del Museo de Bellas Artes y Museo Julio Romero de TorresPlaza del Potro, 1, Córdoba
Dates
1–30 Sept 2026 · Free entry
Opening hours

Open in Maps ↗Route →

Look up. Then look within.

A rain of petals falls over the patio, but not at random: it follows the sacred geometry that orders the walls, lattices and arches of all of Córdoba.

The material looks fragile, yet it endures: blossoming as a form of resilience. The piece is completed by an interactive experience on the city's sacred mathematics.
