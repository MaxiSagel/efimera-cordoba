How it works

  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us who you are, which venue interests you and what you want to show. Until 28 February 2027.

  2. 02

    Selection

    The artistic direction reviews every proposal and chooses the participating companies: 15 installations across seven venues.

  3. 03

    Create the installation

    With an artist of your choice, one we suggest, or in-house. A production assistant supports you, free of charge.

  4. 04

    September, open to all

    From 1 to 30 September 2027, free entry. It comes down on the 30th.

With an artist or in-house

a.

With your artist

Bring the artist you've always wanted to work with. We'll support the project.

b.

With an artist we suggest

If you don't have one in mind, we'll find artists who suit your brand, your material or your technique.

c.

In-house

Your design, R&D or product team signs the work. Ideal for showing what you can do.

How we choose

15 places, one selection. The artistic direction weighs every application against these five criteria.

  1. 1

    The idea

    Originality and strength of the proposal.

  2. 2

    Dialogue with the place

    An installation that understands and respects its heritage setting.

  3. 3

    What only you can do

    How your material, product or technology lives inside the work.

  4. 4

    The experience

    What visitors live: interaction, journey, surprise.

  5. 5

    Feasibility

    That it can be produced, installed and removed without leaving a trace.

01

Patio de la Asociación de Amigos de los Patios Cordobeses

In the heart of the San Basilio neighbourhood, a living emblem of the Cordoban patio: whitewashed walls, flowerpots, lime-washed stairs and historic details.

Hosted “Bajo la fresca” in 2026 · C/ San Basilio, 44
02

Capilla Mudéjar de San Bartolomé

A 14th-century gem and one of the finest examples of Mudéjar art in Andalusia: geometric plasterwork, glazed tiles and a carved wooden ceiling.

Hosted “Lo que permanece” in 2026 · Plaza del Cardenal Salazar, s/n
03

Patio del Museo de Bellas Artes

On Plaza del Potro, inside a former 15th-century hospital, it links the Museum of Fine Arts and the Julio Romero de Torres Museum. Citrus trees, a stone fountain and Renaissance arcades.

Hosted “Sagrado florecer” in 2026 · Plaza del Potro, 1
04

Casa de las Tazas

A private courtyard with a distinctive character, named after its collection of ceramic cups, plates and traditional pottery set among colourful flowers. A charming, personal take on the classic Cordoban patio.

Hosted “Esperar a la luz” in 2026 · Plaza de las Tazas, 11
05

Palacio de los Angulo

A 16th-century noble residence in the historic core: a stone doorway bearing the family coat of arms and a garden courtyard that reflects the prestige of the Renaissance aristocracy.

Hosted “Leyendas” in 2026 · C/ San Pablo, 31
06

Palacio de Viana

A 15th-century palace with twelve courtyards and a garden, each in its own style: from Renaissance layouts to orange-tree patios, a journey through five centuries of the Cordoban patio.

New venue in 2027 · Plaza de Don Gome, 2
07

C3A · Centro de Creación Contemporánea de Andalucía

Across the Guadalquivir, in the Parque de Miraflores, the building by Nieto Sobejano Arquitectos: large halls, concrete walls and outdoor space by the river for large-scale installations.

Hosted the Efímera Lab in 2026 · C/ Carmen Olmedo Checa, s/nPhoto: JamesNarmer, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0
Your product, inside the work

Here your product isn't looked at: it's experienced.

Installations can be interactive: visitors touch the material, trigger the technology, walk through the piece. It's the best way to show what your products can do.

01

Materials

Ceramics, textiles, wood, stone, metal, glass or new composites, at the scale of a patio.

02

Technology

Light, sound, water, climate, sensors or 3D knitting: systems that respond to whoever walks in.

03

Product

Your product inside the work, used and tried by thousands of people over a month.

04

Story

A signed piece that says who you are better than any advert.

Think big.

Installations that fill an entire patio, cover a chapel or can be walked through. A few ideas to get you started.

Taking part

You don't rent a venue: you sign a work.

Selected companies don't pay rent. They pay for the opportunity to create, with an artist or their own team, an installation in a heritage venue, and to present their brand to thousands of visitors and a list of professional guests.

The financial terms are in the deck, which we send to applicant companies.

EFÍMERA Award 2027

Award for the best installation.

Each edition, a jury chooses the installation that best brings together brand, art and place. The award ceremony takes place at the closing, at the end of September.

What counts

The idea, the dialogue with the venue, the use of material or technology, and the visitor's experience.

Who decides

An independent jury from architecture, interior design and the specialist press.

The prize

A guaranteed place in the 2028 edition and a prominent spot in the festival's communications.

Technical partner

Shima Seiki (Wakayama, Japan, 1962) is a world leader in computerised flat knitting machines. In 1995 it introduced WHOLEGARMENT, the technology that knits an entire piece in three dimensions with no seams, now used for complex forms far beyond fashion, such as upholstery and interiors. At EFÍMERA it shares its technical know-how with brands and artists to take each installation from idea to textile.

Media partner

Dezeen, the London-based online magazine of architecture, interiors and design, is the media partner of the second edition.

Partner company · engineering

AKT II

AKT II is the London-based structural and civil engineering practice that partners with EFÍMERA. Its Associate Director, James Kingman, spoke at the Efímera Lab 2026.

Frequently asked questions

Anything else? efimera@fundacionartdecor.org

Do we need to have an artist?

No. You can bring your own, ask us to suggest one who suits your brand, or do it in-house with your design, R&D or product team.

How much does it cost to take part?

It depends on the venue and the scale of the installation. The terms are in the deck we send to applicants. It isn't a rental: it's the chance to create a work and present your brand through it.

What's included?

A heritage venue for all of September, a free production assistant, support from the artistic direction, meetings with the professional guest list and presence in the festival's communication.

Can we choose the venue?

Tick the ones you like best in your application. The artistic direction assigns each venue based on your preference and the scale of the proposal.

How do you work in a heritage building?

The production assistant reviews the technical proposal, permits and installation with you, so the piece goes up and comes down without leaving a trace.

Can our product be part of it?

Yes, that's the point: materials, technology or product inside the work, ideally something visitors can touch, use or walk through.

Do we need a finished project?

No. Tell us what you want to highlight and a first idea. After selection you develop it with your artist or team, supported by the festival.

When will we know if we've been selected?

In March 2027 we notify every applicant of the decision.
