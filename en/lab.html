Efímera Lab

The international workshop where the works are born. Ten days, entirely in English, at the C3A in Córdoba.

How it works

Prototypes by day, ideas at dusk.

Morning and afternoon: teams, materials, prototypes and reviews. At midday and at sunset: guest talks.

Led by Nina Chaltiel (MuDD Architects), it brings together local, national and international participants with students from London's AA School. Grants were open from 1 to 15 July 2026: three full grants for local students and two production grants for artists, professionals and collectives.
